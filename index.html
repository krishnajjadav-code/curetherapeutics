About Cure Therapeutics

Cure Therapeutics is a therapeutic massage practice planned for Irving, Texas. The clinic focuses on professional massage therapy services, complementary wellness treatments, and recovery-focused care within a collaborative rehabilitation environment.

The practice is founded by a licensed massage therapist with international experience and is designed to grow within a clinical setting alongside rehabilitation professionals. The goal is to provide high-quality therapeutic services, support patient recovery, and build a growing team as the clinic develops.
